# Goal: Harness Universal Reconciliation v1

Implement the active support bundle rooted at:

`.agent/plans/harness-universal-reconciliation-v1/`

Read `original.md`, the effective amendment
`amendments/AM-0001-control-loop-semantic-convergence.md`, `decisions.json`,
`amendments/AM-0002-control-plane-final-phase-gate.md`, `requirements.yaml`,
`amendments/AM-0003-prompt-first-pair-repair.md`,
`task-graph.json`, `verification-matrix.json`, the semantic
audit under `.agent/research/`, and the compiled support-pack manifest before
editing.

Execute dependency-ready slices through completion. Preserve requirement, claim,
task, source, and evidence IDs. Do not reinterpret locked decisions, weaken proof,
write outside owned scope, or use more than two non-recursive workers.

Use real browser/device behavior when it best proves a business flow. Use
deterministic scripts for contracts and negative boundaries. Cap each claim at
two repair attempts.

Ordinary conversation is the primary portable entrypoint. Compile normal prompts,
optional slash commands, CLI/API requests, and native host actions into the same
canonical `WorkRequest`. `/goal` is optional host ergonomics only; its absence is
never a failure and it is never an authority or lifecycle boundary.

When the owner reports a defect or mismatch during or after the plan, enter the
pair-repair lifecycle through that normal prompt: preserve the raw finding,
classify it, compute claim impact, reopen only affected claims in a new evidence
epoch, issue a bounded repair packet, implement collaboratively, and require
fresh proof. Never rewrite historical PASS evidence or invalidate unaffected
claims.

Complete all 22 requirements and all ten slices, including S7A semantic
convergence and S7B pair repair. Do not implement the Control Plane redesign in this phase. It is
the final phase and remains ineligible until complete skill/provider/tool/MCP/
rule/command/host/runtime parity, terminal harness evidence, green required CI,
fresh installed-host receipts, and owner-approved harness closeout.

Do not commit, push, update main, delete branches, remove worktrees, or deploy
until an exact CloseoutReceipt is presented to and approved by the owner.

Stop only at:

- `PASS`: every material acceptance claim has fresh evidence;
- `PARTIAL`: useful verified work remains but a non-authority constraint prevents completion;
- `NEEDS_USER`: a material owner decision or visible manual action is required;
- `BLOCKED`: required authority, credential, capability, or external state is unavailable.

On every checkpoint, update the ledger with completed claims, receipts, current
diff scope, remaining risks, and the next safe action.
