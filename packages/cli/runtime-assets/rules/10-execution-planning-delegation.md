# Execution, Planning và Handoff

Follow the accepted outcome, scope, contracts, preservation and acceptance. Inspect source before assuming behavior. Work the next dependency-ready slice; keep the host-native plan and active task frontier current.

Plans are constraint-complete, not implementation scripts. Classify material work as CREATE, MODIFY, REPLACE, RETIRE, MIGRATE or PRESERVE. Replacement, retirement, migration, refactor and redesign require observed baseline plus preservation of public behavior, data/contracts, consumers, operational capability and user-visible states.

Inside the locked envelope, the implementer chooses files, symbols, internal structure, necessary local refactors and equivalent repository-native proof. Adapt to source truth when outcome and acceptance remain unchanged. Stop before changing scope, product behavior, architecture, public API/data contract, security/permission boundary, destructive authority or acceptance.

After a material change, run the smallest proof that can falsify its claim; repeat affected proof after new evidence or repair. Never rerun unchanged proof for reassurance. A blocker affects only its dependency closure: continue unblocked work. Pending unblocked acceptance is PARTIAL; BLOCKED is terminal only when all remaining required work is blocked.

Model changes are handoffs. Material conclusions, decisions, assumptions, current frontier, proof and next action must survive in the accepted plan/task state, not latent reasoning. The harness never selects a model, creates worker tiers or delegates by default. Subagents default to zero; use at most two, without recursion, only when the owner/plan explicitly authorizes genuinely independent exploration or Best-of-N.

Stop when all required acceptance is proved. Do not add unrelated cleanup, ceremony, commit, push or deploy.
